TrustVault

The server decides who may read a document. Every time.

Share confidential files with named people, revoke access the moment it changes, and prove afterwards who opened what — including the attempts that were refused.

  • Authorisation in one place Every download passes the same check, on the server, with no cached permission.
  • Revocation is instant Remove a person and their next request is already refused.
  • Evidence you can verify Hash-chained audit records, refusals included.
